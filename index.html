    How to fly
    Right thumb: the stick (drag up to raise the nose; on the ground on Pilot, left and right steer the nose wheel below 40 kt; Kid and Learner keep you on the centreline)
    Left thumb: throttle (top = full power)
    Middle: gear, flaps, brakes, and ILS AUTO lands it for you
    Pilot: hold NOSE UP / NOSE DN by the throttle to trim; let go of the stick and it settles on the amber trim speed (T on the speed tape)
    Gamepad: left stick flies, RT/LT throttle, A flaps, B gear, X brakes, right stick trims
